# Benchmark and chaos results — 2026-11-02

Environment: Linux VM (this build machine), Rust 1.99.1, release build.
Scope honesty: these measure the queue + worker engine with an in-memory
backend. They prove the software pipeline's throughput and recovery
invariants; they do NOT measure a real printer, spooler, or PDFium —
end-to-end click-to-first-byte is a Windows-gate measurement.

## bench (native-print-tools bench)
- Sustained: 1,000 jobs enqueued in 17 ms; processed to Done in 101 ms
  (9,900 jobs/sec queue throughput). No loss (1000/1000), no duplicates.
- Burst: 200 jobs enqueued in 3 ms.
- Spec target context: 1,000 jobs/hour/terminal for 8h is 0.28 jobs/sec;
  the queue engine's headroom over the target is ~35,000x. The real
  bottleneck in production will be printers and rendering, not the queue.

## chaos (native-print-tools chaos)
- 300 jobs, ~30% injected transient backend failures, process killed and
  the durable (file) queue reopened with startup recovery 11 times.
- Final: done=300, dead_letter=0, failed=0. No job lost, no job printed
  successfully twice. PASS.
